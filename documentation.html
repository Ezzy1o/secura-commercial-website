Platform workflow

How Does It Work?

SECURA helps organizations protect sensitive information by allowing users to upload documents and identify personal details for anonymization before sharing them. Authorized users can manage, access, and share protected documents, while requests for original files follow a controlled approval process.

01

Upload a Document

Authorized users upload supported documents to start controlled processing.

02

Detect Sensitive Information

NLP pinpoints supported sensitive information across each uploaded document.

03

Review and Anonymize

Flagged details are reviewed and anonymized under policy, ready for sharing.

04

Share Securely

Protected copies reach only departments authorized to receive them.

Original-file access remains controlled

Access to an original document is not automatically available to every department. Requests follow a defined review and approval process.
